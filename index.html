The build

Every part has a job.

  1. 1

    Integrated goals

    A goal set into the front and back panels. No extra frames, no pegs, and the game keeps flowing.

  2. 2

    Rebound mesh

    Tight-grid netting across all eight sides keeps the ball in play and sends it back to your feet.

  3. 3

    Kickboards

    Solid low panels take the ground passes and hard strikes, with Baller Box branding front and back.

  4. 4

    Corner joints

    Orange connectors lock each panel to the next. Push together to build, pull apart to pack.

Questions

Before you kick off.

How long does it take to set up?

The panels push into the corner joints by hand, so there are no tools and no loose screws. Most people have it built in a few minutes once they've done it once.

What surface can it go on?

Grass, artificial turf or a hard court. The photos show it on a garden lawn, in a park and on an indoor floor.

Who is it for?

Young players building their touch, parents who want garden football that doesn't end up over the fence, and coaches who want a rebound station they can move between sessions.

Can two people play at once?

Yes. There's a goal on each side, so it works as a 1v1 cage as well as a solo training wall.

How does it pack away?

Pull the panels out of the joints and everything slides into the Kick Lab carry bag, which has a shoulder strap.
